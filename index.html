Research

Questions that come directly out of my regulatory and planning work in Dhaka.

I am an urban planner and GIS/remote sensing specialist with more than five years of experience at Bangladesh's capital development authority (RAJUK) and in planning consultancy. My work centres on land-use regulation, land readjustment, transit-oriented development and urban regeneration in Dhaka.

At RAJUK I am drafting regulatory rules for land readjustment and densification around transit nodes, and I lead drone mapping of about 120 sq km to detect unauthorised land development. I am trained in data science (M.Sc.) and have co-authored peer-reviewed articles in transport safety.

Projects

Planning tools, policy and plans from my work at RAJUK that are in use in Dhaka today.

  1. Live

    Public Web GIS platform for Dhaka’s Detailed Area Plan

    masterplan.rajuk.gov.bd, built on ArcGIS Enterprise in collaboration with Esri Bangladesh

    Residents can now look up the land use of their own plot from home, and overlay it with flood-prone areas, heritage zones and other planning layers, instead of coming to the RAJUK office. The platform received CityNet’s SDG Breakthrough Innovation Award in 2025.

    My role: Project Manager of the GIS/RS Lab project that built the platform

  2. Live

    FAR calculator for the Detailed Area Plan

    Web app, built in Python (Streamlit)

    Landowners used to visit the RAJUK office to find out how many storeys they could build. With this calculator they can check the permitted floor area ratio (FAR) and building height for their plot from home.

    My role: designed and built the calculator

    Open the FAR calculator

  3. Gazetted May 2025

    Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) Guidelines for Dhaka

    Bangladesh Gazette, 28 May 2025, S.R.O. No. 187-Law/2025, Ministry of Housing and Public Works

    Approved by the government under the Town Improvement Act, 1953, the guidelines apply across RAJUK’s 1,528 sq km Detailed Area Plan area and link land use, density and design to Dhaka’s mass transit corridors.

    My role: central contributor to the Project for Development of Policy and Guidelines for TOD along Mass Transit Corridors

  4. Featured by the Gap Fund

    Climate-Resilient Green Action Plan (CRGAP) for Hazaribagh and Lalbagh

    RAJUK with GIZ and the City Climate Finance Gap Fund (GIZ and EIB)

    A roadmap for building climate adaptation and mitigation into the regeneration of two old Dhaka neighbourhoods. Planners worked with residents to map water flows, climate risks and social vulnerability. For Hazaribagh the plan proposes twelve smaller blocks with more light, open space and greenery; for Lalbagh, better drainage, shaded public space and infrastructure that can withstand floods and heat.

    My role: spatial analysis, stakeholder mapping, ward-level participatory appraisal and workshops

  5. Ongoing

    Heritage mapping of Dhaka

    Mapping Dhaka’s heritage sites together with the history that shaped the city.
